Research

  1. Feb. 2026 – Present

    LLM Fine-Tuning and Evaluation

    CBU AI & Machine Learning Lab (CAIMLL) · Riverside, CA

    • Built the backbone of the lab’s fine-tuning pipeline: complex reference documents become source-verified training data, with checks for evidence coverage, answer quality, and reproducibility.
    • Designed training examples that separate fully answerable, partially answerable, and unsupported questions, so models learn to admit knowledge gaps while answering what they can.
    • Developed distributed adapter and full-parameter fine-tuning workflows, and audited synthetic data for wording and topic shortcuts.

  2. Aug. 2025 – Present

    Subliminal Learning in LLMs — Study Lead

    California Baptist University · Riverside, CA

    • Led a controlled study comparing LoRA-induced and system-prompt-induced teacher preferences, fine-tuning Llama 3.2 3B students on filtered numerical sequences.
    • Neither pipeline produced robust preference transfer under the tested conditions; leading the manuscript through revision.

  3. Jan. 2026 – Present

    Hydrology — Statistical Rainfall Estimation

    Research collaboration with Dr. Benjamin Sanders

    • Modeled hourly rainfall transfer from CSUSB to Panorama Point using 1996–2024 gauge records; event-calibrated lagged OLS reached R² = 0.58 on held-out observed event hours (≥ 0.04 in/hr).

  4. Jul. – Oct. 2025

    AI Researcher — Mechanistic Interpretability

    Algoverse AI Research Program · Remote

    • Implemented JAX/Haiku transformers for RASP-style algorithmic tasks and sparse-autoencoder pipelines to analyze attention and MLP representations.
    Code on GitHub →

  5. Mar. 2024 – Sept. 2025

    Student Researcher — GeoAI for Public Health

    California Baptist University · Riverside, CA

    • Trained a deep neural network to predict geographic areas of suspected drug-overdose risk, and built the GIS visualizations in Kepler.gl that turned its output into insights; coauthored the AMCIS 2025 paper.

  6. May 2024 – Aug. 2025

    Undergraduate Research Assistant — Computational Physics
